PortWatch · flasher

AnyPS5 progress desk display — firmware b1.0. Use Chrome or Edge on Mac or Windows.
This browser can't talk to USB serial devices. Open this file in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1 Pick your board

Drop PortWatch-*.bin here or click to choose it

2 Flash

Plug the board in with a data USB cable. If it won't connect, hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then try again. On a Mac with a CH340 board, close any serial monitor first.

3 Wi-Fi & display

Sends your Wi-Fi details and display settings to the board over USB. You can skip Wi-Fi here and use the on-screen setup network (PortWatch-XXXX) from your phone instead.
